# Glarion brand guidelines

## Positioning

Glarion is **website security monitoring and client-ready reporting for digital agencies**.

The primary promise is: **Know what changed before your client does.**

Use the category, audience and outcome together near the start of public material. Glarion monitors websites from the outside, triages automated findings and turns them into an agency-branded client deliverable. It complements maintenance dashboards; it does not update plugins, take backups or replace a penetration test.

## Messaging order

1. Category: website security monitoring.
2. Audience: digital agencies managing client websites.
3. Outcome: see meaningful changes early and report them clearly.
4. Proof: public check, sample report, authorization controls and precise product behavior.
5. Commercial model: transparent plans by site allowance.

Prefer evidence over superlatives. State what the product does, how a control works and where its boundary lies. Do not call Glarion comprehensive, enterprise-grade, guaranteed, AI-powered or best-in-class without specific, current evidence.

## Voice

- Calm, exact and useful. Write for an agency owner and the technical person who will verify the claim.
- Use short sentences and concrete verbs: check, verify, compare, review, report.
- Explain security terms at first use. Never use fear as the opening argument.
- Avoid inflated promises, adversarial competitor copy and generic “peace of mind” language.
- Label hypothetical commercial math **Revenue illustration — example, not a promise** and state that results vary.

## Product language

Use “public check” for the ungated preview that reads DNS, TLS, homepage headers, robots.txt and security.txt. Use “full scan” for active, plan-gated scanning of a verified domain. Use “finding” for an observation that needs action or review. Use “report” for the self-contained, agency-branded HTML deliverable that can be printed to PDF.

Do not describe the public check as a vulnerability scan. Do not describe an automated full scan as a penetration test, certification or guarantee of security. Never imply Glarion can scan arbitrary third-party targets.

## Visual system

Use a dark, restrained interface with high-contrast type and structural rules.

- Background: `#0B0B0C`; surfaces: `#11161D`, `#171D26`
- Primary text: `#F5F7FA`; secondary: `#B7C0CC`; quiet: `#919DAB`
- Rule: `#303844`; clear: `#62D5A0`; caution: `#F3BD62`; alert: `#FF7665`

Use status colors only when they carry meaning and pair each with a label. Maintain visible keyboard focus, semantic headings, labelled fields and practical touch targets. Respect reduced-motion preferences. Keep the supplied mark undistorted and do not recolor it.

## Calls to action

The experiential CTA is **Check a website**. The proof CTA is **View sample report**. The account CTA is **Start free** or **Create an account**. Never suggest the public check requires an email or card.

## Canonical references

Brand name: **Glarion**. Domain: **glarion.app**. Do not abbreviate the name or introduce a second product name. Check pricing, allowances and security behavior against the application and repository before publication.
