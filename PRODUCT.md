## Platform

web

## Users

Repository-supported working assumption, awaiting optional user clarification:
digital agencies and freelancers managing client websites.

## Product Purpose

Monitor verified client websites, prioritize findings and provide client-ready reports.
Success means visitors understand the public check's limits and can try it, then
evaluate scheduled monitoring for their client portfolio.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Rust/Axum/Postgres API, vanilla TypeScript dashboard, static marketing pages.
Public checks read DNS, TLS, homepage headers and public policy files.
Full scans require a paid plan and current ownership proof at queue and execution.
Weekly/monthly schedules, change notifications, downloadable agency reports.
Retain current plan prices, authentication, public-check behavior, logo and English content.
No claim of complete security, guaranteed revenue or substitution for a manual penetration test.

## Evidence on Hand

README.md, backend enforcement and tests, sample-report.html (fictional sample).
No verified customer testimonials or conversion benchmarks supplied.

## Product Principles

- Make public-check limitations visible before and after use.
- Keep the report's evidence attached to its recommendations.
- Use measurable acquisition experiments, without invented outcomes.
- Preserve existing services and behavior while changing presentation.
