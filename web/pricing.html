Questions

What counts as one site?

One domain added to your account. Free allows one site; Solo five; Studio ten; Agency forty. Full scans require current proof that you control the domain.

Is the free plan a trial?

No. It does not expire and it never asks for a card. The public check needs no account. A Free account can hold one site. The check reads the certificate, security headers, SPF and DMARC, cookie flags and public policy files. It does not include the full scan, scheduling or branded reports.

Do the prices include VAT?

Prices exclude VAT. Any applicable tax and the final total are shown in Stripe before you pay.

How do I pay, and how do I cancel?

Checkout and cancellation are both hosted by Stripe, so no card detail reaches Glarion's servers. The same Stripe billing portal — reachable from your account — changes the card, switches plan and cancels. Cancelling stops scheduled checks at the end of the period you have paid for.

What happens when my domain verification expires?

Proof of control lasts 30 days, and it is checked again at the moment a scan runs rather than only when it was queued. If it has lapsed, the scheduled scan is refused and says so, rather than quietly scanning on. The site stays in your account; prove control again and the schedule resumes.

Is there a limit on how often a site can be scanned?

Yes, and it applies on every plan including Agency: six full scans per site per rolling 24 hours. It is not a plan feature to be upgraded past. This limit controls scan traffic and applies equally to all paid plans.

Is a Glarion scan a penetration test?

No. Glarion provides automated monitoring and evidence from external checks. It does not replace a scoped manual penetration test or guarantee that a website is secure.

Can I put Glarion in front of a site I do not own?

The public check, yes — it reads only what the site broadcasts to every visitor and every search crawler. The full scan, no. It runs only after DNS or hosted-file proof that you control the domain, so checks run only on domains you are authorised to manage.
