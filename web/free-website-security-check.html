What the public check requests

It reads the HTTPS homepage, /robots.txt and /.well-known/security.txt, looks up SPF and DMARC in public DNS and opens a TLS connection to read the certificate.

It does not guess paths, test vulnerability fingerprints, submit forms or attempt a login. Redirects are not followed; response sizes and request times are limited. If something cannot be read, the result says so.

For details or to block these requests, see About our checks.

Common questions

Is the free website security check really free?

Yes. It needs no account. Paid plans add full scans on verified domains, scheduled monitoring and branded reports.

What does the public check request?

The HTTPS homepage, /robots.txt and /.well-known/security.txt, public DNS for SPF and DMARC, and a TLS connection to read the certificate. It does not guess paths, submit forms or attempt a login.

Can a clean result prove my site is secure?

No. A partial check reports what a site publishes; missing settings may need review, and their importance depends on the website.
