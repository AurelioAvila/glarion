Why expiry matters

When a certificate expires, browsers may warn visitors or block the connection. The expiry date gives your agency a concrete maintenance task to discuss with the hosting provider.

Automatic renewal can fail. Confirm both that renewal is configured and that the replacement certificate is being served to visitors.

What Glarion checks

A TLS connection reads the certificate presented by the checked server. This can help reveal a renewal that succeeded on the origin but has not reached the proxy or edge serving visitors.

The free public check flags certificates with fewer than 30 days remaining. Full scans increase priority at 14 and 7 days.

Keep renewal and monitoring working together

Each weekly or monthly scan reads the expiry again. Email notifications are sent when scan results change. Scheduled scans require current domain verification.

The schedule determines when a change is observed; it cannot guarantee advance warning. Keep automatic renewal and your hosting provider’s expiry alerts enabled.
