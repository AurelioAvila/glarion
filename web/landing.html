A few things to know.

What is free, and what needs a paid plan?

The public check gives you a partial view of a website’s public configuration, with no account or card. Paid plans add full scans, scheduled monitoring and agency-branded reports. What the free check covers.

Why do I need to verify my website?

Full scans actively check for known vulnerabilities, so you must prove control of the domain first. Add a DNS TXT record or a hosted file. Verification lasts 30 days and is checked again before scans run.

Can I use my own name and logo?

Yes. Paid reports carry your agency’s name and optional logo. Download them as HTML or use your browser to save a PDF. See a fictional sample.

Does Glarion fix issues for me?

Glarion shows findings and suggested next steps. Your team handles the fixes. Keep your existing tools for updates, backups and uptime.

Does a scan prove my website is secure?

No. It is an automated check of what can be observed from outside, not a manual penetration test or a security guarantee. Read the scope and limits.
