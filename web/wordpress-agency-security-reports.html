Make security work visible in your WordPress care plans.

Your client needs to know what you checked, what deserves attention and what changed after the work. Glarion helps agencies turn website observations into reports with their own name and logo.

The sample report uses a fictional client. Glarion is an independent website assessment tool, not a WordPress plugin or a WordPress-endorsed service.

Start with the scope you can defend

Record the client’s domain, the permitted checks and the date of the assessment. Use Glarion’s domain-verification workflow before running checks that require proof of control. Authorization to maintain a WordPress installation should not be treated as permission to test every service belonging to that client.

Review the public-facing observations in context: certificate configuration, security headers, email authentication and the checks included in your chosen assessment. Keep the report’s scope and limitations alongside the findings so a client can distinguish a detected issue from an area that was not assessed.

Connect a finding to the maintenance task

An expired certificate may belong to the hosting team; an email-authentication finding may belong to the domain administrator. Assign the next action to the team that controls the relevant setting. Explain the observed condition, its likely effect and the evidence needed to confirm the correction.

For a WordPress care plan, keep core, plugin and theme updates, backups, access reviews and recovery testing in your maintenance checklist. Glarion’s external observations do not establish that every plugin is current, a backup can be restored or a site is free of malware. Verify those tasks through the appropriate WordPress and hosting workflows.

Send a report the client can use

Agency-branded reports are included on Glarion’s paid plans. Use your name and logo, preserve the assessment date and explain the next step in plain language. Severity describes a finding; business priority also depends on the site’s exposure and the client’s operations.

After a change, repeat the relevant check and compare the observation with the earlier report. Keep implementation and recovery records in your maintenance workflow. Glarion reports observations; it does not apply WordPress fixes or guarantee that a change resolved every related risk.
